ACME CORP ENTERPRISE INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

1. Access Control & Passwords
- All employees must enable Hardware FIDO2 Security Keys (YubiKey) or TOTP MFA on Okta.
- Passwords must be at least 16 characters and rotated every 90 days if compromise is suspected.
- SSH keys for production servers must use Ed25519 with passphrase protection and 8-hour max certificate TTL via Teleport.

2. Data Classification
- Level 1: Public (Marketing material, public documentation).
- Level 2: Internal (Internal runbooks, Slack communications, project tickets).
- Level 3: Confidential (Customer PII, employee payroll, SOC2 audit logs). Encryption at rest (AES-256) and TLS 1.3 in transit mandatory.
- Level 4: Restricted (API Master Keys, Vault root tokens, customer encryption keys). Multi-party approval required for access.

3. Remote Work & Device Security
- All company laptops must run CrowdStrike Falcon EDR and full-disk encryption (BitLocker / FileVault).
- Connecting to public unencrypted WiFi networks without the corporate VPN is strictly prohibited.
